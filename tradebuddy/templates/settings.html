Written market summary (TB-AI)
A Mistral model id, e.g. ministral-3b-2512.
5 is the floor. Each run is a paid request per symbol.
This is the only feature that sends anything off this machine. Every 5-15 minutes the measured numbers on Market Data — price, volatility, the counted direction, the levels above and below, the volume profile and the estimated order flow — are sent to Mistral, which writes them up as sentences. No balance, no position, no order, no P&L and no key are ever included; the fields that may be sent are an allowlist in the code, not a habit. The answer is badged WRITTEN on the page because it is the one thing there that cannot be rechecked against a bar: the model computes nothing, decides nothing and never places an order. It stays off unless MISTRAL_API_KEY is set in .env — the key is read from the environment and is never stored in the database.
Daily Email Report
Once a day at this hour (in the "Trading day starts" timezone) the engine emails what happened today: PnL, every trade and order, a timeline, why signals were skipped, trailing stops, open positions, accounts and errors. A failed send is retried twice, 15 minutes apart; each attempt is in the Event Log. Gmail: port 587 (or 465) with an App Password. Send any day by hand from the Trading Journal.
