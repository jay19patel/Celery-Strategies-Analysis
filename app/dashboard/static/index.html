What This System Does

This system runs several independent trading strategies (…) on …, fully automatically, on a fixed schedule. Each strategy trades with its own simulated $100 paper account — they never share money or affect each other. No real money is ever used; every trade here is a simulation used to compare how each strategy actually performs.

The Full Cycle — Step by Step

1⏰ Celery Beat Scheduler Fires
Every 10 minutes, the scheduler automatically kicks off a fresh batch run — no manual trigger needed.
↓
2🧮 Every Strategy Runs in Parallel
For each symbol, every strategy independently fetches the latest price data and decides: BUY, SELL, or HOLD.
↓
3🛡️ Protective Exit Check (Stop Loss / Target)
Before anything else, every currently open position is checked against the latest price. If the price has crossed its stop-loss or take-profit level, that position is closed immediately — this happens every cycle, even if the strategy itself says HOLD.
↓
4🚦 Did Any Strategy Signal BUY or SELL?
The system checks whether anything actionable happened this cycle.
↓
❌ No — All HOLD
Nothing else happens. System waits for the next cycle.
✅ Yes — Actionable Signal
Continue to Step 5 below.
↓
5💰 Paper Broker Opens / Closes / Reverses the Trade
That strategy's own $100 (or whatever it has grown/shrunk to) account is used to open a new position — or close/reverse the existing one — using 100% of its current balance.
↓
6🗄️ Saved to the Database
Updated balance, open position, and (if a trade closed) the full trade record — entry, exit, stop, target, PnL, reason — are all saved.
↓
7📊 Dashboard Updates
The Overview, Analytics, and Trades tabs on this dashboard reflect the change automatically the next time they refresh.

Every Strategy Has Its Own Separate Money

No strategy ever touches another's balance. Wins and losses stay within that strategy's own account and compound over time.

A Position's Lifecycle

1. Opened100% of current balance is used to enter
2. MonitoredChecked every cycle against stop & target

3b. Closed — Take Profit HitPrice reached the target level
